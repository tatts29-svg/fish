The standards behind the numbers

Double scan - two scans, two looks, one standard. Every issue and every return goes through the tool store: scan, look. Scan, look. The scan is not complete until we have looked - so the record above is evidence, not estimate. Right first time. Every item. Every time.
Checking and stocktakes. Stock takes are done daily - no exceptions - with nothing in the store going more than 30 days unscanned. 3013 of 4135 items physically verified in the last 3 days (73%) by 4 counters. Discrepancies are addressed on the spot, and the stock take doubles as our on-hire verification - anything physically in the store still showing as on hire gets corrected the same day. It is not just a counting exercise; it is another checkpoint for catching problems before a hirer does.
Safety-critical assurance. 72 radio handsets (70 billable, 2 spares held) and the 30-unit gas monitor fleet are pooled, charged and maintained through the store - every gas monitor bump tested and fully charged before it is issued, and every battery item issued on a full charge, never a partial. Nothing leaves the store that we would not use ourselves. Life Saving Rules (SEQ-GL-009) apply: Rule 5 - Tools and Equipment - damaged or defective gear is tagged out and removed from service, never reissued.
Pride in what we do. Best Service & Value is the objective; the disciplines above are how the K2 tool store delivers it, every shift.
