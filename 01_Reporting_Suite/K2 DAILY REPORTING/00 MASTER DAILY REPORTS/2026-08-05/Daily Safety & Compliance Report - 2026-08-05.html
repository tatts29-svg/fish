Safety-critical gear gets a different level of care, and this report is the evidence. Milwaukee tooling is tracked piece by piece for its high replacement value. Life Saving Rules (SEQ-GL-009) sit under all of it - Rule 5, Tools and Equipment: anything damaged or defective is tagged out, photographed and removed from service on the spot. Never left on the shelf. Never reissued.
