What still works with no internet

  • Everything in the store. My Gear and the stores board are files on this laptop, served over the store's own Wi-Fi. Workers' phones do not touch the internet to read them.
  • Every report button. They build from the exports already in Data_SiteIQ — no signal needed.
  • The label printer. The Zebra prints over the store network.
  • This pack. Printed paper needs nothing at all.

What does not

  • Pulling a fresh SiteIQ export (button 01) — that is the one job that needs the uplink.
  • Submitting charges back to SiteIQ — they queue and go when the line returns.
  • Emailing the packs — drafts still build, they just sit in Outlook until you have a signal.

So the plan is simple

  1. Keep trading. Use the ISSUE and RETURN logs in this pack — every movement gets a name against it on paper.
  2. Tell the crews the phone page is a snapshot from 02 Aug 2026 - 20:45 and anything since is on paper at the window.
  3. When the line comes back: pull a fresh export, type the paper in, then run the day as normal. The catch-up page at the back walks it through.

  6. Pull ANOTHER fresh export and run 00_RUN_EVERYTHING. The day's reports, the phone page and the board all rebuild off the corrected data.
  7. Check button 40 on both laptops — same version, same numbers.

Keep the paper
