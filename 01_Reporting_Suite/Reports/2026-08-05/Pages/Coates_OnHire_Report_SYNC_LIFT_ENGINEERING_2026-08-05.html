The tool store has your back

Scan & look - double scanned, every time
Every item is scanned and sighted going out, and scanned and sighted coming back - our scan-and-look process. If it moved, it is on the record. That is your protection as much as ours.
Stock you can trust
The store runs stock rotation with full stock checks every 3 days - what the register says is on the shelf is what is on the shelf.
SDS on your phone
Safety Data Sheets are available inside the tool store, and outside it - scan the QR code on the wall and the SDS downloads straight to your phone.
Logbook gear
Anything marked LOGBOOK needs its daily pre-start and logbook entry before it operates - books and blank pages are at the counter.
Breakdowns - tell us everything
Something stops? Report it straight away with the details: where it is, what is wrong, what it was doing. We will get it swapped or fixed - no drama, no waiting.
Floggers & the small stuff
Using floggers? Flogger holders are available at the store. How-to cards, radio channel cards and gas monitor guides live on the counter - just ask.
