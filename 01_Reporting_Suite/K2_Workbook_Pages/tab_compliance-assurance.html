K2 SHUTDOWN — COMPLIANCE & ASSURANCE
Cement Australia Gladstone | 20 Jul – 11 Aug 2026 | Coates Shutdown Toolstore
Coates' Role — Equipment Management On Site
Coates manages the equipment fleet on site end to end. Every piece of gear that comes back through the toolstore is checked in, cleaned, checked for compliance and checked for safe use before it is reissued. Nothing goes back on the shelf unverified.
Inspection & Test Compliance
Electrical equipment:COMPLIANT — Test & tag current across the fleet. Current period tag colour: BLUE (June – August 2026).
Rigging equipment:COMPLIANT — Quarterly inspection current across the fleet. Current period tag colour: BLUE (June – August 2026).
All electrical and rigging gear issued from the toolstore carries a current BLUE tag. Out-of-period tags are quarantined at return, not reissued.
Every Return, Every Time
1. Check in:Scanned back to the toolstore — the on-hire registers and company accountability update on refresh.
2. Clean:Cleaned before restocking — gear goes back on the shelf ready to issue.
3. Compliance check:Test tags, inspection currency and bump-test status verified against the BLUE period.
4. Safe-use check:Inspected for damage and defects. Anything unsafe is tagged out and removed from service (Life Saving Rule 5) and led as an exception in the daily report.
Daily Return Targets — End of Shift, to the Toolstore
Gas monitors:Return daily for bump test, charging, inspection and reissue.
Radios & batteries:Return daily for charging, inspection and radio availability.
Milwaukee batteries:Return daily for charging and rotation.
General tooling:Return daily for inspection, charging and availability.

Stock checks:Stock checks are completed in store and recorded in the Stocktake Log tab — SiteIQ holdings and the physical toolstore stay aligned throughout the shutdown.
SWMS sign-on:No high-risk task commences without a completed risk assessment and sign-on to SWMS-CTS-001 (Life Saving Rule 2).
Escalation Ladder — Outstanding Equipment
Day 0–1:Return Today — standard daily return expectation.
Day 2:Overdue — Medium priority; holder contacted.
Day 3:High priority — supervisor follow-up.
Day 4+:Critical — Long Term Outstanding; escalated with replacement cost visibility.
Value Protection
Hire charges:Gas monitors $29.75/day and DP4801e radios $12.81/day are charged for the full fleet daily, whether issued or in the toolstore. All other equipment is charged per the contracted rates when on hire.
Replacement exposure:Every on-hire line carries replacement cost visibility — general tooling schedule $999,028.69 across 3,897 units, plus current Milwaukee market pricing (~$145.8k across 287 units).
Price discipline:Estimated replacement prices are quote-gated — nothing is charged to Cement Australia without a verified supplier quote.
