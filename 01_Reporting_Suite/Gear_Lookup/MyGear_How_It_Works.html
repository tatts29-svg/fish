HOW IT WORKS

Every morning

Tool store — about 15 minutes
  1. Pull today's numbers — run 28_PULL_SITEIQ_EXPORTS.bat - it takes the SiteIQ exports out of Downloads and files them
  2. Build the page — run 04_RUN_MY_GEAR.bat
  3. Serve it — run 05_START_GEAR_LOOKUP.bat and leave that window open - close it and the QR codes stop working
  4. Check the address — if it prints something other than the address at the foot of this sheet, run 32_UPDATE_POSTERS.bat and reprint the posters

Getting your list

Anyone on site — about 30 seconds
  1. Join the store Wi-Fi — scan QR 1 on the poster, tap join — or join Tool Store by hand, password coates2026
  2. Open My Gear — scan QR 2, or type the address at the foot of this sheet
  3. Put your ID in — type your hire ID - the number on your Coates card
  4. Your gear appears — everything in your name, oldest first, with what each item needs
  5. Take it with you — print the A4 sheet, or just keep it open on your phone

Updated once a day

The list is built about 7:00 AM from that morning's SiteIQ pull. Anything taken or handed back after that shows up tomorrow. It is a record of what is in your name, not a live feed — if it looks wrong, check the time before you chase it.

If something looks wrong

Where do I type my numberStraight on the page when it opens. Your hire ID is the number on your Coates card.
Page won't openThe store server isn't running. Ask the tool store to start it.
Gear I handed back is still listedThe list is built once a day at 7:00 AM. It'll be right tomorrow.
Gear I took this morning isn't thereSame reason - anything after 7:00 AM shows tomorrow.
It says nothing is in my nameWrong ID number. A number that isn't yours shows an empty list, never someone else's gear.
