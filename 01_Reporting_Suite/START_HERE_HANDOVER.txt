WHAT THIS SUITE IS
  One folder (01_Reporting_Suite) that turns the morning's SiteIQ
  exports into every report the K2 shutdown needs: company activity
  packs, the exec summary, demob, consumables, stocktake, safety,
  returns, the phone pages on the store Wi-Fi, and an Outlook draft
  for every one of them. One button (00_RUN_EVERYTHING.bat) runs the
  whole morning. NOTHING SENDS ITSELF - every email is a draft a
  person reads and presses Send on.

THE MORNING, IN ORDER
  1. Save fresh SiteIQ exports into Data_SiteIQ\ (same names, over
     the top). Close the K2 workbook in Excel.
  2. Double-click 00_RUN_EVERYTHING.bat.
  3. Everything lands in Reports\<today>\ - Pages, PDF, Emails,
     Excel_Backups. The day's company packs land in
     K2 DAILY REPORTING\01 COMPANY REPORTS\<company>\<today>.
  4. The reports you SEND are the ones in Reports\<today> - open the
     drafts, read them, send. Extra attachments come from
     Reports\<today>\PDF.
  5. Nothing goes to a client until CHECK_REPORTS says PASS.

THE STANDING RULES - NEVER BEND THESE
  * NO money, revenue or rates on anything served over the store
    Wi-Fi. Money lives only behind the manager code, separately
    encrypted (.k2m blobs, XOR/mulberry32 keyed from the code).
  * Tracked and client-owned gear carries NO dollar figure anywhere.
    Not even $0. A rate on gear that is not ours is not ours to print.
  * Totals must SAY how many lines they left out - counting them as
    nothing is a lie by omission. A cut list that says nothing is a
    lie by omission too.
  * One company's email NEVER names another contractor.
  * The main Worker phone menu must not reach everything; anything
    with money is manager-login only.
  * Nothing sends itself. Drafts only.
  * All work is committed and pushed to branch claude/new-session-kz0u8o
    - never any other branch.

THE EXPORT QUIRKS THAT COST US DAYS
  * Every SiteIQ export (RENTAL_STOCK, ON_HIRE, TRANSACTIONS,
    STOCKTAKE, SALES_STOCK, DAILY_SUMMARY) has a REFERENCE_INFO sheet
    FIRST and the data on the SECOND sheet. Read sheet 2. The pull
    timestamp lives on REFERENCE_INFO (REQUESTED_DATE/TIME) - use it,
    exports pulled the same morning can be hours apart, and
    departures.py depends on that ordering.
  * Fleet_No = Item_Number; Serial_No is that item's serial
    (Andrew, 3 Aug). Placeholder serials ("Coates1302153", "TBA") are
    listed in SERIALS_TO_FIX.csv, they are not data.
  * STOCKTAKE rows with STATUS "Pending Branch Receipt" + ACTION
    "Departure" are gear that has LEFT SITE - departures.py builds the
    index; a departure timestamped after the register pull wins, one
    before it (on gear the register still lists) means it came back.

THE BUG CLASSES THAT KEEP COMING BACK
  * .format() over a string containing CSS braces dies on
    {box-sizing}. Keep braces and .format() in separate rooms.
  * Non-raw Python strings eat escapes - r"" anything with
    backslashes.
  * Email clients do not render SVG. Outlook hands it to Word, which
    draws NOTHING. Charts must go PNG via email_images.svg_to_png(),
    inlined by Content-ID (add_related, never add_attachment).
  * SVG text needs an explicit font-family or headless renderers draw
    no axis labels at all - it looks like a design choice. It is not.
  * Headless Chrome gives the page ~40px LESS than --window-size and
    pads the screenshot white. svg_to_png() renders taller and crops
    back (reserved_height() measures the exact gap per machine).
  * Inside a <table>, only <tr> may follow </tr>. A loose <div> gets
    hoisted OUT by the parser and draws outside the frame. _e_* row
    helpers exist for exactly this.
  * _e_note() escapes its LABEL (plain text) - "&mdash;" in a label
    ships as literal text. Use the real character u"—".
  * Local repo sometimes rolls back to an old commit on container
    restart. Fix: git fetch origin claude/new-session-kz0u8o &&
    git reset --hard FETCH_HEAD. Never rebuild history.
