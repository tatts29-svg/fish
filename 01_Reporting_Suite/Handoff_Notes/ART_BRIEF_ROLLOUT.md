# Art brief → MyGear rollout

The workbook defines 80 named art surfaces: 19 declared live and 61 still to build. Its `IMAGE MATCHES` sign-off cells are blank, so the existing 19 should be visually verified before being treated as approved.

## Rollout order

1. **Catalogue foundation — complete**
   - Bind all 1,171 exact variant codes to their approved external JPG filenames.
   - Use the shared charcoal `#0A0E14`–`#14181F` world, Coates orange `#F26222`, white top light and orange floor/rim glow.
   - Preserve `FAMILY` status and the item-identity warning.

2. **Worker journey and access scenes**
   - Finish the three missing journey/access scenes.
   - Use the same real-equipment visual language from sign-in to item detail.

3. **Visual store**
   - Build the 21 category tiles from the approved thumbnails; do not substitute flat icons.
   - Prioritise the six aisles that cover 4,819 of 5,375 live catalogue lines (89.7%): Tooling, Electrical, Rigging, Welding, Air and Radios.

4. **Location wayfinding**
   - Complete the 14 aisle/location surfaces using recognisable equipment silhouettes and one consistent bay language.

5. **Store Street**
   - Approve one master storefront system, then extend it across all 14 report fronts.
   - Keep third-party branding, people, prices and readable labels out of generated scenery.

## Acceptance gate for each surface

- Correct destination and object class.
- Reads at thumbnail/card size.
- Matches the locked dark/orange MyGear world.
- Contains no fabricated brand, serial, batch, expiry, rating or price.
- Keeps written item identity usable when the image fails.
- Records the sign-off in the workbook instead of relying on memory.
