 WHAT IT DOES

   Turns three SiteIQ exports into the shutdown on-hire report.
   Every time you press it you get two things:

     * the workbook  - twelve tabs, every line behind the numbers
     * the email     - the whole report in the body, ready to send,
                       with the workbook already attached. NO COSTS.


 WHAT YOU DO - three steps, about a minute

   ------------------------------------------------------------------
   STEP 1     Put today's exports in the Data_SiteIQ folder
   ------------------------------------------------------------------

              Pull these three out of SiteIQ and save them into
              Data_SiteIQ, straight over the top of yesterday's:

                   RENTAL_STOCK
                   SALES_STOCK
                   TRANSACTIONS

              STOCKTAKE as well if you have it - not required.

              Don't rename them. SiteIQ's own long file names are
              fine (RENTAL_STOCK_10_08_2026 06_05 AM.xlsx). If there
              is more than one, the newest is used.


   ------------------------------------------------------------------
   STEP 2     Double-click  REFRESH_THE_REPORT.bat
   ------------------------------------------------------------------

              A black window opens and counts the tabs out. Takes
              about ten seconds. When it says DONE you can close it.

              If something is wrong it tells you in plain words what
              to fix. It does not build half a report.


   ------------------------------------------------------------------
   STEP 3     Open what it just made
   ------------------------------------------------------------------

              THE WORKBOOK, in this folder:

                   <Job>_Onhire_Workbook_LATEST.xlsx

              Always the newest, always the same name - so a
              shortcut to it on the desktop never goes stale.
              Start on the Cover tab.

              THE EMAIL, in Reports\<today>\ :

                   <Job> On-Hire Report - <date>.eml

              Double-click it. It opens in Outlook as a DRAFT with
              the whole report laid out in the body and the workbook
              already attached. Put the addresses on, read it, press
              Send.

              NOTHING SENDS BY ITSELF. Ever.

   ------------------------------------------------------------------

   That's the whole job. There is no step 4.


 WHAT'S IN THE EMAIL - and what isn't

   The position          items on hire, companies holding gear, the
                         fleet on site, issues and returns
   Who is holding gear   every company, what they still have out and
                         what hasn't come back
   Working hardest       the gear earning its keep, and what to do
   Not moving            the biggest holdings that haven't gone out
                         at all - shelf space we're carrying
   Consumables to watch  the lines running down
   Out the longest       oldest items still out, worth a phone call

   NO COSTS. Not the labour, not the cost breakdown, not a rate and
   not a dollar sign anywhere in it. That is deliberate - the email
   is the operational picture and it can go to anyone. The money
   stays in the workbook.


 WHAT COMES OUT - twelve tabs

   Cover                    what this is, what fed it, the headlines
   Company Summary          per company: tooling, consumables, total
   Detailed Onhire          every item out right now, and who has it
   Tooling Transactions     every tooling issue and return
   Tooling Utilisation      per item type: turns, rating, what to do
   Coates Tooling           the Coates-owned fleet on site
   Consumable Transactions  every consumable issued
   Consumables Available    what's left on the shelf
   Coates Stock             the fleet with its on-hire dates
   Consumable Utilisation   per line: usage, rating, what to do
   Coates Labour            the shift roster       - TYPED BY HAND
   Cost Breakdown           the day-by-day cost    - TYPED BY HAND


 FIRST TIME ON THIS COMPUTER

   Copy the whole folder across. All of it, not just the button.

   The computer needs Python 3. If it hasn't got it, the button says
   so and tells you where to get it - python.org, the big yellow
   Download button, and TICK "Add python.exe to PATH" on the first
   screen. Two minutes, once, and never again.

   You do NOT need Excel to build the report. Only to open it.


 THE TWO TABS THAT ARE TYPED BY HAND

   Coates Labour and Cost Breakdown are not built from the exports -
   somebody types them. The refresh NEVER touches them, and neither
   of them appears in the email.

   Keep your working .xlsm in this folder and every refresh reads
   those two tabs out of it and writes them straight back, formulas
   and all. Your .xlsm is never written to.

   No .xlsm in the folder? Those two tabs come out empty with a note,
   ready to type into. Nothing breaks.


 A DIFFERENT JOB

   Copy this folder, empty Data_SiteIQ, put the new job's exports in,
   press the button. It reads the job off the exports themselves, so
   it names itself correctly with nothing set up.

   Open JOB.txt only if you want to tidy up the names it uses.

   ONE JOB'S EXPORTS AT A TIME. If a Gladstone export ends up in a
   Weipa folder it stops and names the odd one out, instead of
   quietly building a report off two jobs mixed together.


 WANT MORE DETAIL?

   Open HOW_IT_WORKS.html - one page, prints on A4. It shows the
   flow end to end, what each tab is for, and how the Usage Ratings
   are worked out.
