The day's emails - one click, then Send

Everything is already wired. Your only job is press the buttons in order, check, and hit Send. Nothing ever sends by itself.

The morning (it's already part of RUN EVERYTHING)

1
00_RUN_EVERYTHING.bat - the one button. It builds every report, every company pack and every email draft. If you ran this, steps 2 and 3 below are ALREADY DONE - skip to 4.

Or piece by piece

2
17_BUILD_DAILY_EMAIL_PACKS.bat - builds today's company folders: the addressed draft, the report PDFs and the record. Every address comes from K2_Daily_Email_Report_Allocation.xlsx - nothing typed into code.
3
18_CHECK_DAILY_EMAIL_PACKS.bat - the 203-check prover. It re-opens every pack and proves the right report is in the right email to the right company. Anything doubtful is HELD and named - a held pack cannot be sent by accident.
4
Open Outlook - the drafts are sitting in your Drafts folder, report pasted into the body, PDF attached, addresses filled. (If they're not there, run 12_MAKE_OUTLOOK_DRAFTS.bat with Outlook open.)
5
Read it like the client will. Right company? Right day? Looks right? Then press Send. That's the whole job - you are the only send button in this system.
Rule of the house: nothing sends without YES at 06_SEND_TODAYS_REPORTS.bat / your press of Send in Outlook. The suite builds and proves; you decide.
