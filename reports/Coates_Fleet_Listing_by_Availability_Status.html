Executive summary — the position

▲RED — ACTION NEEDED Redline is 16.26% against a <15% target

Of 21,492 assets on hand across 40 QLD and NT branches — $336,502,703 at original cost, $199,981,145 written down — 3,495 are unavailable. That is 16.26% of the fleet against the Coates Way benchmark of under 15%, or 271 assets too many, with $32.03M of written-down value sitting out of service.

Inspection Pending is the single driver. It accounts for 2,417 of the 3,495 redline assets (69.2%), and 859 of those have been in that status for more than 90 days — the longest at 1,289 days. Clearing the inspection backlog alone would take redline below the benchmark.

The exposure is concentrated, not network-wide. Branches of 100 assets or more with the highest redline share: WOOS 38.4%, EAGS 34.5%, MEAD 30.9%, GYMP 26.3%, MRBH 25.9%. Across the network 1,345 redline assets have been stalled beyond 90 days, holding $7.64M of WDV. By category, Compaction carries the largest redline value at $5,734,411 (21.5% of that category unavailable).

Utilisation. 11,525 assets (53.6%) are on hire, carrying $127.87M of WDV. Read this as a point-in-time on-hire share of units on hand, not as measured time utilisation — the 65% Coates Way target is the time-based measure and the two are not interchangeable. A further 1,636 available assets have not moved in over 180 days.
