## v6.68 (machine v5.85) — 26 Sep 2026 — the dyno run you can see, a safety control officer, nothing blocks the view

Andrew: "make sure everything is up to date … when we move around nothing blocks the visual view … high detailed and very smooth … we need to see the wheels running at high speed when we throttle it … a safety control officer in this area who makes sure everyone is safe … and what on earth are those connected to the tyres at the back."

- **The things on the rear tyres** were the dyno's two exhaust-extraction hoses. Their funnels met the side pipes where the pipes end — right at the front of the rear tyres — so they read as hoses on the tyres, and they hung across the view. They're gone (and their exhibit boxes with them).
- **Wheels at speed.** The throttle only ever revved the V8: the box sat in neutral, and the machine's inspection drive turns everything slowly on purpose. Now, with the V8 running and the throttle open, the box goes into first by itself, climbs a gear on the limiter, and drops back to neutral after a few seconds off the throttle (a shift by hand in the last 12 s always wins). The rear wheels and the dyno rollers spin up to the road speed the dials show — capped at 38 rad/s where the eye can still follow — and a blur disc fades over each rear wheel as it gets fast. Tested: 1st engages, spin 20 → 38 rad/s in ~6 s, gears climb.
- **Safety control officer**: a seventh crew member in a hi-vis vest with SAFETY front and back, patrolling the aisle round the cell and stopping to watch the car; while the V8 runs he holds the line on the near side, watching the rear wheels and rollers. His card: keeps everyone out of the line of the rollers and wheels, checks the zone before a start, watches every lift and wheel change, can stop the job (Life Saving Rule: Risk Assessment).
- **Nothing blocks the view** (`view-fx.js`): anything in the hall between the camera and what it's looking at — columns, gantry, racks, the forklift — fades to a ghost while it's in the way and comes back after. The car, V8, cog and people never fade.
