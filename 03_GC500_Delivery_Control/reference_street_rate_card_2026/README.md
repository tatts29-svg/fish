## What it settles

The toilets. $90.07 hire + $36.44 install + $36.44 demob + one $72.87 pump-out = **$235.81**. This year's card splits
the hire from the labour and the pump-outs, so the contracts' Rate 1 on the FWF lines ($90.07), the accessible toilets
($337.75) and the 16-pan unit ($2,852.13) are the card's own hire figures and are right. The page charges the labour per
piece and the servicing per visit as their own lines, which is the card's logic. Nothing on those lines is a question.

Two kitties the card names that the page does not carry yet: **damage waiver** (hire only, rate from the branch) and
**transport** (the card has no transport line; the contracts carry delivery and pickup lines). Both are this year's
questions and sit on the Questions page as such.
