# P&L terminology and duplicate presentation — draft

Author: Andrew Fisher.

Andrew asks for correct business terminology, concise data and no repeated financial summaries. This draft is presentation-only and must follow v9.80. It is not ready to publish: Andrew is providing a current P&L example for the final presentation review.

The visible audit found repeated Revenue, costs and labour summaries. The legacy glance called a subtotal including overheads “Direct costs”. Its current difference also excluded priced wages while the reconciled branch difference included them. The draft retains the financial models and removes the redundant glance, labels the three different Revenue scopes explicitly, keeps overheads/wages separate and uses “Difference so far — not a margin yet” for the existing reconciled branch result. Onsite Installation and cleaning are kept together under an accurate combined label; hire includes both Hire and Rehire Revenue.

Existing components, source rows, financial controls, unknown rates and reconciliation failures are retained. Supporting prose and the labour breakdown use More info. Source screenshots and native data are kept outside Git. The older July 2026 P&L guide and AGENTS terminology were read; this does not claim review of Andrew’s incoming example.
