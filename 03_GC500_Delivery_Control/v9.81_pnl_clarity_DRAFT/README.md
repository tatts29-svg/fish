Author: Andrew Fisher.

The statement follows the terminology and hierarchy in Andrew's Coates P&L examples. Only the format is reused; their branch names, figures, rates and period are excluded. Customer V8 Supercars Aust Pty, customer code V8SU0845 and site QLD_GOLD COAST 600 appear once in each financial view and Finance export.

One whole-job statement replaces repeated summaries. Existing branch, source and wages breakdowns open under More info. Finance retains distinct supplier/contract-line identities and its PO receipt controls. An unsupported supplier-PO label is removed from the salary allowance display/export; its cost is retained. Accommodation and meals remain separate from labour.

Onsite equipment has its complete applicable priced charges now, including future physical cleaning, demob and pickup. That subtotal is not added again to the whole-job forecast. Whole-job Current record plus Additional forecast equals Job forecast. Visible cost-basis wording identifies estimates and commitments; these are not asserted to be receipted/posted actuals. Gross Margin uses known direct costs; Difference so far includes priced wages and overheads.

Andrew's pasted emails and clarification reinforce October cost capture, demob forecasts by branch, and PO/branch/receipt detail. No email is sent, historical deadline year inferred, receipt invented or journal claimed posted. Source rates, receipt/value gaps, unpriced labour/transport and export-snapshot limits remain explicit. The whole-job Finance table includes workers' home branches; adding them to the separate monthly hours export remains outside this presentation patch.
