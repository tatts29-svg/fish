Author: Andrew Fisher

Andrew requested a map picture and unfinished jobs, added navigation information, whole-area sharing, and a picture showing where the equipment is within the location. This release adds Share map + jobs to the selected reference and Share area + jobs to the selected area in the main Servicing map.

The prepared picture includes the selected servicing night, verified-progress time, site overview, closer registered-map view, labelled locations and unfinished tasks grouped by reference. Crowded labels use non-overlapping callouts with lines anchored to the original equipment positions. It reuses the Servicing map's equipment icons, recorded coordinates, area colours and area-envelope functions. Separate equipment spots within one reference have matching labels on the map and job list. Pump out, Clean & restock and Water checked / refilled retain the existing task rules. Directions use recorded navigation pins; missing positions are identified. The user chooses a recipient in the device share menu, or saves the PNG and copies the jobs/directions. No record or message is sent automatically.
