# The fencing crew's print-out — "What you built" (GC500 2026)

Author: Andrew Fisher. Andrew, 24 Oct 2026: "the boys have asked for a print out of what they have done. As in fencing could you
put something together. Something that looks really good they want to know how much they have done ... Something neat".

`GC500_2026_fencing_crew_what_you_built.pdf` — two A4 pages, print at actual size (also scales cleanly to A3).

- Page 1: 20.8 km of fence and crowd control; 13.3 km temporary fence (653 m over the programme), 7.3 km crowd control,
  4.4 km scrim; the gear (5,223 panels, 41 hoarding panels, 8,235 feet, 7,977 clamps, 1,426 braces, 2,903 barriers, 133 + 22 gates);
  the crew (Wayne Woods, supervisor, and the nine names on the record); a thank-you from Andrew.
- Page 2: metres by week with the hire agreements each week; record breakers; how the feet add up; the work done twice;
  the weather; 233 signed papers.
- Corrected 24 Oct 2026 on Andrew's word ("Concrete block are wrong ... are they not feet"): the first version had a
  "216 concrete blocks" tile; those were the feet on docket 36566. The tile now shows the 41 hoarding panels.

No money anywhere on it. Every figure is the live record's own reading on 24 Oct 2026 (record 10804, page v11.81.1), the same
figures as the v11.81 Advanced account and the race call. Weather: Bureau of Meteorology, Gold Coast Seaway, 7 Sep–23 Oct 2026,
as cited in the v11.65 race-call script.

**The feet (Andrew, 24 Oct: "One pannel at each end go into the feet. And bracing get 3 feet i think").** The dockets show two
feet a brace, not three: every bracing-only docket has exactly two bases per brace (36519 29/58, 36531 42/84, 36566 108/216,
36581 29/58, 36763 17/34 ...). Panels take one foot each, shared at the joins. 8,235 bases are written on the dockets; 300 of
them were a drop-off at the iEDM compound (36600, 36620) and 19 sit under flat-feet barriers (36591); 11 papers left the feet
box blank (230 panels, 15 braces, about 260 feet). From the gear: 5,264 panels (5,223 mesh + 41 hoarding) + 2 × 1,426 + 300 + 19
= 8,435, plus one foot at the open end of each run — about 8,495, the same as the dockets plus the blanks. There are no
separate concrete blocks: the 216 "fence blocks" were docket 36566's feet, inside the 8,235. The print shows the docketed 8,235 (as the race
call says) and explains the rest.
