## The layout to build (v12.60, Codex): the Costs tab, top to bottom

1. **Forecast P&L — one table.** Columns: Line · KINP · STPS · NVAC · MEAD · Job (no branch) · On the record ·
   Job end. Rows: the revenue lines above (fire extinguishers stay inside Hire as today; drop the $0 Damage waiver
   row unless it carries a figure), then Revenue; the cost lines: Rehire · Toilet pump-out costs · Consumables ·
   Transport (carriers) · Installation external · Labour, wages · Labour hire (Job Connect) · Accommodation ·
   Meals and travel · Misc, then Costs; then Margin $ and %. Every cell a figure or "—". No sentence on the face.
   A branch cell is the line's amount on that branch from the data (the contract line's branch, the reference's
   branch, the person's hours on that branch's references), never a fixed weight. A figure that is an estimate or
   an allowance carries "est." and its basis lives in the workings. The two by-branch tables of today become this
   one; "Branch split reconciles" is a single tick under the table.
2. **Not in it yet** — a short list of what is missing with its estimated dollar effect, nothing else.
3. **Workings** — one closed fold holding everything that is prose today: the basis sentences, the recovery
   ratios and their words, Rehire by branch, Revenue by branch and the 2026 card, On the contracts by the rate,
   the card's estimate comparison, Charge checks, Installation revenue and wages, What the job will cost to the
   end (its figures are the Job-end column), September accruals, Finance journals, Charge lines on the record,
   Ticks and lock, Site vs Baseplan to align.
4. **Fencing, everything included** — moves out of the top of Costs to the fencing section after the P&L
   (Andrew, 29 Oct: "fencing is still in here up the top why?"). It was put at the top on 27 Oct (v12.42.23)
   for the fencing reconciliation; that job is done.
5. **Customer rates and charges (the street rate card, card748)** — its own fold at the bottom, "Rates", where a
   rate is changed; nothing of it on the P&L face.
6. **Transport (cartage) typed lines** — the card goes; it has no lines and never had revenue. Transport is the
   two P&L rows above, by branch.
7. **Workforce** — the P&L carries the four rows (wages · labour hire · accommodation · meals and travel) by
   branch; the Workforce tab keeps the roster, the hours and the purchase orders. Labour hire shows hours now and
   dollars when the invoices land on the orders.
