## Placement and implementation limits

Andrew's earlier placement notes remain useful context: Boostmobile near pit entry, GOLDCOAST near
“Breaker Street” (his wording), and the other event board at the start of the stretch. These are approximate
user notes; this photo review does not turn them into verified street names, coordinates or dimensions.

The two Boost sign arrangements and three bridge designs above must not be collapsed into one generic gantry.
Match structures using their supports, adjoining barriers, facade groups and the existing circuit data. Repeated
views strengthen identification; filename order alone does not establish distance, direction or lap closure.

The MP4s remain useful for corner/kerb shape, racing context and continuous movement. Their older-looking sponsor
treatments are not authority to overwrite the signage in these newly supplied references. Neither source is a
survey of 2026 geometry. Do not add parked cars, traffic diversions, access-day cones or construction hoarding as
permanent racing features without a separate reason.

Next implementation work should use the photographs to refine the existing full-lap environment: facade depth,
varied foliage, concrete joints/scuffing, transparent mesh, distinct event structures and local shadows. Preserve
the existing circuit, car, driving simulation, camera choices, media/weather and controls. Exact landmark placement
remains a matching task before edits; no guessed gantry coordinates are authorised by this index.
