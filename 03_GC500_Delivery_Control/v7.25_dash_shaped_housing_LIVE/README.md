# v7.25: the Where we are panel shaped like a real dash (LIVE)

Author: Andrew Fisher

Andrew, 28 Sep 2026: "Make it look like a dashboard mate, like the shape and all. Obviously yours needs to be better."

The v7.24 instrument panel now sits in a moulded dash housing instead of a rounded box. The housing is drawn in real pixels at the panel's own size, and redrawn only when that size changes, so every edge stays exact on a phone and at 4K.

**The housing:**
- a cowl rising over the top on sloped shoulders;
- sides that flare out towards the bottom, like a dash fascia;
- a dark moulded bezel around a separate inner instrument face, with a fine seam, an inner lip and a faint sheen under the cowl;
- a bevel highlight on the outer edge;
- an orange perimeter that's bright along the top and fades down the sides, with a soft glow;
- a soft two-layer drop shadow.

**The gauge** gains a thin glass bezel ring and a faint reflection.

**On the phone:** the flare and cowl scale down, and the browser's automatic text enlargement is switched off for the panel, so its text stays at the intended size.

Figures, drawers and motion are unchanged from v7.24.

Tested:
- 0 errors at 3840×2160, desktop at 1× and 2×, tablet, and phones at 390 and 360 px;
- the full sweep of all tabs, on desktop and phone;
- the money probe is identical;
- pre-starts still print one page each;
- rapid switching, date changes and a redraw with no entry replay all behave.
