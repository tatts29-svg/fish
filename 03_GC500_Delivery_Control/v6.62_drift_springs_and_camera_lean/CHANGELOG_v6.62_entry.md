## v6.62 — 26 Sep 2026 — every move, drift and slide, and the camera that sells it

- Andrew: "improve the motion of every move and drift and slide. And angle of camera to like a wow wow factor."
- Motion (`G.step`): the slide is now a damped spring — the tail steps out under power, runs a touch past its angle and is caught, instead of easing to a fixed angle and parking there. Roll and pitch are springs too, so the car squats, dives and leans and rocks back once when the load comes off; the body now leans about two degrees at the cornering limit (it was a tenth of that). The front wheels steer: the corner's own angle less the slide, so a drift shows opposite lock and they straighten as it is caught (±18° at most).
- Camera: close rigs lean with the slide and roll (a dutch tilt up to about 5°), the driver's view rolls with the cockpit, the drone banks gently as it circles; a director cut cuts the tilt too. Close shots widen with speed and throttle (up to ~12 %) and tighten on the brakes. Chase and low ride an arm — the car pulls away on the throttle and comes back under brakes. Trackside, grid, car-follow and brake-attack cameras carry a little hand-held float. Reduced motion turns all of it off.
- Applies to every vehicle (the plant and the towed VMS slide on the same springs; the trailer follows the hitch).
- `test_v662.js`: 40 s of the sim stepped at 120 Hz — slip peaks 0.38 rad inside its limit, steering runs +0.32 to −0.08 rad (opposite lock in the slides), roll 0.036 rad, pitch 0.023 rad, camera tilt 0.085 rad, lens up to +12 %; everything finite; no script errors. `shots/` has a slide from four rigs.
