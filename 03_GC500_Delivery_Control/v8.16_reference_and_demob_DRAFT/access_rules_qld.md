# Demob road rules: what the Queensland Access Conditions Guide says

Author: Andrew Fisher. Source: Queensland Access Conditions Guide, Version 6.0, December 2023 (TMR), sent by Andrew on 3 Oct 2026.
The guide covers **oversize heavy vehicles only**: over 4.5 t operating under a notice or permit (s1, s4). A normal toilet truck that is within
standard dimensions is not covered. These rules apply only to a load that is flagged as oversize. The page never guesses which loads are oversize.

Before relying on this, check that 6.0 is still the current version on the TMR website.

## Rules the Demob tab applies to a load flagged oversize

| Rule | What it says | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Gold Coast peak hours | Any oversize vehicle in the Gold Coast City Council area, on a business day: **no travel 07:00–09:00 or 16:00–18:00** | s11.2, Table 3 |
| Weekends and public holidays, wide or long loads | More than 3.1 m wide or 25 m long, in the southeast corner (Gold Coast included): **no travel from 07:00 to sunset on Sat, Sun or statewide public holidays**. Also no travel from 12:00 to sunset on the day before a statewide long weekend | s11.3, Table 4 |
| No convoys | Oversize vehicles must not run in convoy. Keep 200 m behind another oversize vehicle unless on a multi-lane road or in a built-up area. Stagger the departures | s9 |
| Speed | 80 km/h if a pilot or escort is required, 90 km/h if not, or the posted limit if lower | s5, Table 1 |
| Permit | A permit is needed above 4.5 m wide, 35 m long or 5.0 m high (critical roads and areas), or above 5.5 m wide elsewhere. The permit must be carried in the vehicle | s7, s12.2, Table 12 |
| Night | Rigid truck: night travel only up to 3.1 m wide and 12.5 m long. Any oversize vehicle may keep going until 19:00 if it set off in daylight and can finish by then | s13, Tables 13–14 |
| Before every trip | The operator and driver must check the TMR Conditions of Operation Database and current road closures (QLD Traffic) | s10.2, s10.4 |
| Cranes and SPVs under 3.5 m wide | May travel in Gold Coast peak hours only if travelling to or from a job site | Schedule 1, Table 15 |

## What this means alongside site hours (07:00–17:00, Andrew)

On a business day, an oversize load can only be **on Gold Coast roads from 09:00 to 16:00**. That window sits inside site hours.
A loaded oversize truck must be off site by about 16:00 minus its travel time to the edge of the council area. The run sheet shows this as a
latest departure time. **Travel time stays an input. It is not assumed.**

The demob window is Mon 26 Oct to Fri 13 Nov 2026, and it runs on weekdays only. The weekends in that window (31 Oct–1 Nov, 7–8 Nov) are
not demob days. As far as I know there is no Queensland statewide public holiday in the window (King's Birthday was Mon 5 Oct 2026), but
please confirm.

## What the guide does not give us

- **Pilot and escort numbers** (Tables 9–11): these tables are images in the PDF and could not be extracted. The page shows "check permit"
  and never shows a count.
- **Travel times**: these come from Andrew or the transport supplier, never from the guide.
- **Which loads are oversize**: these are flagged per load by the branch.
