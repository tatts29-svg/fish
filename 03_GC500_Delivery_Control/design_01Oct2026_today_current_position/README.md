# Design — Today · Current Position (proposal, no page change)

Author: Andrew Fisher · 1 Oct 2026

Andrew's brief (1 Oct): merge the two top-level views, Today and Where we are, into one landing page, "Today — Current
Position", that answers "what do I need to act on?" in under ten seconds, keeps the Coates language (dark command
rail, warm paper, orange as the accent only, numbered 01/02/03 sections, 9px 0 geometry, factual wording,
provenance and caveats), replaces the two tabs with one "Today + position" destination, adds a section jump rail
that writes the fragment, and shows each measure once. Detailed trade, branch and revenue tables stay under
disclosure or on their own tabs.

`Today_Current_Position_design.html` is the visual design: a framed mock of the unified page on the live record's
figures as at 04:40 on Thu 01 Oct 2026, numbered design-note pins, a what-moves-where table, the one-source-of-truth
rules, and the phone treatment. Published as a private artifact for Andrew to review. **Nothing on the live page
changes until Andrew says yes.**
