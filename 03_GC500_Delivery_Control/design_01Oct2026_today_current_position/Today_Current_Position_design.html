Design notes

1

The command rail stays

Deep charcoal-brown rail, Coates identity, search, the Gold Coast clock, the race-day countdown and the LIVE record light are kept as they are. The tab row loses Today and Where we are and gains one active destination, Today + position. Orange stays on the active tab only.

2

Section jump rail

A slim rail under the introduction sticks to the top as the page scrolls. Each link scrolls to an anchored section and writes the fragment (#today, #deliveries, #position, #programme, #workstreams, #commercial, #records), so a link to a section still lands on that section. The active link is underlined in orange as the sections pass.

3

Act on now

The exception list is the ten-second answer. It carries the six unrecorded due items, the 17-unit shortfall (once, as a pointer to Current position), the road reports and the look-ahead. Wording stays factual: "no delivery record", "not recorded on site", never a bare colour.

4

One delivery-against-plan component

93.1% appears once, as the big figure with its scale. The 17 units sit beside it in a red-ruled block with the caveat that a gap in the record is not proof of lateness. The 3 early units are named as a separate fact. Provenance (58 asset numbers, 1 sub-hired machine, 69 lines set on site) sits under it.

5

Detail under disclosure

Fencing by line, the trade table and the branch table open on request or on their own tabs. The one line that is behind (braced for scrim, 215 of 2,492 m) is called out in the headline card so it is not buried under the disclosure.

6

Provenance everywhere

"As at", last confirmed, planned versus observed and the incomplete-rate warning are kept in place: in the introduction, on the position component, on the programme ("calendar position only") and on the revenue figure.

One source of truth

  • 93.1% — one component, in Current position. Nowhere else.
  • 17 units — beside that component, and once in Act on now as a pointer. Not a third headline.
  • Due today 6 in · 0 out — Daily brief and the Deliveries board. Not in the Programme section.
  • Trades, branches, revenue make-up — under disclosure controls or on Plant, Fencing and Costs. The unified page carries their headlines only.
  • Wording — "recorded on site", "due by this day", "no delivery record", "a gap in the record, not proof they're late". No green/amber/red labels without the fact beside them.
