Author: Andrew Fisher

All selectable restraint families were checked against the original Coates Load Restraint Guide 2023. Every family remains reference/recording support, with automatic restraint approval disabled. Equipment dimensions and mass in catalogue tables describe the named model, not every GC500 unit with a similar description.

Implemented hard bounds catch known contradictions but are not a complete compliance engine. All 16 families return `fits:false` and `restraintValidated:false`; numerical planning output uses `geometryFits` separately. A verified actual specification is neither a vehicle approval nor a restraint design.
