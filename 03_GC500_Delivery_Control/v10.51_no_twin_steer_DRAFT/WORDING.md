## The wording (used on screen, in the print check and on every printed page)

**NO TWIN-STEER TRUCKS — GC500 SITE**

Twin-steer trucks must not be used for any GC500 delivery or pick-up. That means nowhere on the track, the Esplanade
or inside Macintosh Island Park.

This applies to every delivery and pick-up in **Week 3, Week 2, Week 1, Race Week and demob**:

- **Week 1:** there is next to no room on site.
- **Race Week:** the track is more enclosed and runs one way only.

**If a twin-steer truck turns up**, it will cause delays on site. There is nowhere to park and nowhere to unload, and
it may not be able to unload at all.

**Before you print:** confirm that no twin-steer truck is booked for these loads. You can't print run sheets or dockets
until you tick the box.

> ☐ I confirm no twin-steer truck will be used for any load on this paperwork.
