## What it tells us about the lines on the P&L

1. **The toilets' hire is Rehire** (2127, the manual-cost twin of 2126 Re Hire Contract Costs). Same as this year's page
   treats it.
2. **The servicing — and the water truck with it — is Toilet Pumpout Costs** (3325, in the Transport group of the P&L),
   not Rehire. So the Revenue for the servicing belongs on **1032 Hire Revenue – Toilet Pumpouts**, in the Transport
   group, as the P&L guide read it. The water truck goes with the pump-outs, not with the deliveries.
3. **Water deliveries and the drinking-water tank are Consumables** (2144 Consumables – Held for Resale), so their
   Revenue belongs on **1020 Hire Revenue – Consumables**, and the ratio the business reads for them is **Consumables
   Recovery**. This year's four water lines split the same way: the water truck and the pre-fill with the pump-outs
   (3325 / 1032); the deliveries and the tank as consumables (2144 / 1020).
4. **Last year's servicing was bought by the week** (three weeks of 260 services, 17 cleans and 8 pump-outs); this year's
   Q6844 is 780 services, 51 cleans and 24 pump-outs — the same shape, three weeks folded into one quote.
5. **Last year Coates paid Event Portables $96,340 ex GST** on these three quotes; this year's four approved quotes are
   $118,575 ex GST. The gap is this year's larger toilet count and the longer hire, not a rate change: the unit prices
   match line for line (Fleet Fresh Flush $105, accessible $350 → this year $300, pee panel $450, VIP combo $2,750,
   16-pan $2,750, FWF service $35 → this year $38.50, block clean $165 → $185, tank pump-out $205 → $295, water truck
   $6,800 both years).
