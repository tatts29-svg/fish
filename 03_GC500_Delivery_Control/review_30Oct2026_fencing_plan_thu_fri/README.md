  invoices are the truth for work done (Andrew, 22 Oct 2026). No paper is altered or inferred from it.
- **What to look for in the next pile**: Thursday's Triangle (140 m and 200 m of CCB), S01/S02 stairs (175 m) and Club 500

  strip north collection (450 m, 342 m CCB), the Helen Park infill agreement (130 m) and the walkthroughs (200 m).
- **What moves the charge to the V8s**: only clean metres on hire agreements, at the 2026 card (this batch runs at $16.16 a
  metre, 90 m = $1,454.46): S07's 36782 once its metre is confirmed (140–150 m, about $2,260–2,420), S08's 65 m (about $1,050)
  and Helen Park's 130 m (about $2,100) when their papers come. Removal and relocation metres do not price; Advanced bills
  those by the hour, and the plan gives the scale of the hours to expect: 1,119 m plus 380 m of CCB out on Thursday, 650 m
  plus 342 m on Friday.

  stands; this plan is the operational side of that line.
