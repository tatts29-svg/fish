# Installed-circuit imagery

Author: Andrew Fisher.

Andrew asked for satellite images showing the track set up. Two dated aerial sources were verified on 2 October 2026. Neither is a verified event-day satellite capture or evidence of the 2026 equipment positions.

| Source | Capture | What is visible | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Surfers Paradise Street Circuit aerial view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Surfers_Paradise_Street_Circuit_aerial_view.jpg) | 26 October 2019 | Installed barriers, pit straight, long pit/hospitality structures and event facilities | 4032 × 3024 helicopter photograph by MasterMind5991, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Suitable as a credited historical visual reference; oblique and not a georeferenced map layer. It does not show the whole circuit. |
| [Gold Coast 2014 10cm SISP LGA](https://qldspatial.information.qld.gov.au/catalogue/custom/search.page?q=%22Gold_Coast_2014_10cm_SISP_LGA%22) | 15–16 October 2014 | Overhead pit/hospitality structures, bridges and event staging during installation; ordinary traffic remains | Official 10 cm aerial orthophotography. Partial setup, not completed race-day proof. Individual metadata lists shared IP and a Single Supply Licence; redistribution permission was not verified. Link the official source and keep research exports private. |

The 2019 photograph's capture date is from its original metadata, distinct from its 27 October upload date. The Queensland source is ImageServer item 3974, project metadata C154D739-9255-4A3E-ACD6-9115082F0523.

The existing Queensland 2022 survey was captured between 24 July and 12 September and shows ordinary streets and parkland. It does not establish an assembled GC500 circuit. The existing plan's faded aerial has no independently verified capture date. Google's copyright year is not a capture date.

No new imagery layer is included in v8.13. The master plan D001-26003-03 and current system records remain authoritative for locations. Historical photographs can inform visual details, but must not silently move current equipment or be presented as current satellite coverage.
