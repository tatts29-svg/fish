⛔ NO TWIN-STEER TRUCKS —
GC500 SITE
Twin-steer trucks must not be used for any GC500 delivery or
pick-up. That means nowhere on the track, the Esplanade or
inside Macintosh Island Park.

This applies to every delivery and pick-up in Week 3, Week 2,
Week 1, Race Week and demob. Week 1: there is next to no
room on site. Race Week: the track is more enclosed and runs
one way only.

If a twin-steer truck turns up, it will cause delays on site. There is
nowhere to park and nowhere to unload, and it may not be able to
unload at all.
