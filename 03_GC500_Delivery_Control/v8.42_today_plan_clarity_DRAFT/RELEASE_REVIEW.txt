Resulting behaviour
- Today retains its motorsport housing with wider laptop instruments. Total,
  Done, Left, completion percentage and dated plan position are brought forward.
- A known total remains useful when completion evidence conflicts. Confirmed
  completion is a rounded-down lower bound, clearly labelled; Left includes
  work requiring review. Detail rows retain that qualification and known count.
- On-site plan counts use scheduled active references with explicit arrival or
  completion evidence. Rental starts alone are insufficient. Early arrivals
  cannot erase other references due but still missing. This comparison concerns
  arrival dates; it does not invent installation deadlines.
- Fencing shows every supported Build work type in its existing instrument,
  with its own Total, Recorded, Left, percentage and dated plan comparison.
  Metres and gates remain separate. Over-plan work is identified; overlapping
  fence work is not combined into a physical track-completion percentage.
- Source-authored dates remain unchanged. Invalid or unreconciled programme
  coverage is not presented as confirmed progress. Relevant source conflicts
  retain useful numeric comparisons with an explicit provisional qualification.
- Broader type, whole-programme/Demob, source and cost detail remains available
  within the matching instruments. Docket labour is labelled as that column;
  it does not claim to include separately recorded crew hours.
- Matching Total/Done/Left drilldowns, selected-date replay, print expansion,
  focus, fold state and automatic motion remain connected. Decorative motion
  runs on one visible selected instrument and respects Pause, reduced motion,
  hidden views, dialogs, printing and global motion settings.
