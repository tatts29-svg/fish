v1.0 - 12 Aug 2026
  - First stand-up of the Ampol suite on the K2 pattern.
  - One Data\ area for every Excel; every engine reads from it.
  - All six report families under numbered buttons, plus the
    two new register reports (Calibrations, Rigging).
  - Dated Reports\ output, drafts-never-send, CHECK gates.

v1.1 - 02 Sep 2026  (gas monitor report rebuilt - accuracy)
  - Every gas monitor number is now counted by gasmon_engine.py from
    the two SiteIQ exports (RENTAL_STOCK = where every monitor is,
    TRANSACTIONS = every issue and return). The Excel workbook is no
    longer a source of numbers - it is attached to the email if it is
    in Data\, and refreshing it is optional.
  - Three windows, labelled on every page: year to date, last 30
    days, yesterday. Who does not bring them back, by name and by
    company, with the year-to-date column beside the 30-day one.
  - "Recovered today" is counted (it was hard-coded to zero, which
    pinned the Returns score at 0/100 every morning). The score now
    uses the 30-day same-day return rate.
  - Custody and workflow accounts (Dräger statuses, FCCU, Operations,
    Future Fuels, After Hours) are never named as people.
  - A data-and-method page prints the sources, the rules and the
    reconciliation between the two exports on every build.
  - The build measures every page in the browser before printing and
    shouts if content will not fit (the print engine drops a chart
    that does not fit without changing the page count).
  - Button 02 (executive dashboard) and the email read the same engine
    - three outputs, one set of numbers.
  - Three windows on every people and company table: last 30 days,
    last 3 months (the 13 full weeks before this one plus this week)
    and year to date. A "where these numbers come from" page up front
    sizes each window so a big count can be traced to its rows.
  - The workbook is no longer attached to the email (its summary tab
    is what the old report quoted). attach_workbook in
    generate_k2style_email.py turns it back on.
  - Serial alignment is checked on every build: 821 of 878 monitors
    carry a serial on the list, 57 do not (gap list handed to Andrew).

v1.2 - 02 Sep 2026  (every family on the raw exports - no workbook refresh)
  - Rule for the whole suite: the three SiteIQ exports (RENTAL_STOCK,
    TRANSACTIONS, STOCKTAKE) plus the small lookup files are the only
    sources. No report needs a workbook opened and refreshed.
  - Radio report (03): every count from RENTAL_STOCK - on hire this
    year, prior years, out of service, available, exposure. Prices from
    the pricing master (unpriced shown as a dash). Serial coverage is
    the honest ratio (627 of 634). Ampol_Radio_Report.xlsm is not read.
  - Stocktake (05): chargers, probes and joiners are no longer counted
    as gas monitors (daily-three tile now 324 of 335 in-store monitors,
    not 324 of 516). "Last 24 hours" is a real 24-hour window. Departed
    stock is excluded only when the live register has let it go too
    (17 rigging-bay items came back onto the shelf list). Serial-
    numbered monitors priced from the master's own family line and
    disclosed. SOP compliance prints its in-store / on-hire split;
    activity charts say "items by latest sighting"; company rows merge
    the register's spellings and flag repairs / Dräger custody;
    "$0" and "TBC" now read "unpriced".
  - Tooling (04): every number counted from the three exports plus the
    pricing master and description mapping - the workbook is not read.
    One on-hire total on every page (1,500 = Q1 140 / Q2 544 / Q3 816;
    the old pages carried four different totals). Available 4,053 and
    repairs 70 from the live register (stale tabs said 4,337 / 91).
    Utilisation computed at the report date, with the priced /
    unpriced split on the spend tile. One company rule on both sides;
    repairs and Dräger custody never a company or a person; 853
    pre-2026 rows disclosed; September marked partial; Q4 skipped
    until it starts.
  - Rigging (07): the register workbook is read only for what is typed
    into it; every status, hirer and held-days figure is joined live
    from RENTAL_STOCK. 905 rows = 889 distinct barcodes (1 blank, 14
    repeated); 746 found in SiteIQ, 143 not found on their own pages;
    301 on hire to 14 customers, 13 at repairs; the workbook's
    certificate extract (Next Insp Due 04/26 on 375 items) disclosed
    as unconfirmed.
  - Calibration (06): the register is read only for the due dates
    typed into Register Entry; status buckets, chase list, on-hire and
    "on hire, not in the register" are computed from RENTAL_STOCK at
    its pull time. At 02 Sep: 135 in cal / 6 due inside 30 days / 48
    overdue / 158 no date (the register's own 14 Aug view - 137/26/26
    - is printed beside it, labelled). Chase list 8 across 6 hirers.
    A banner says how old the due-date entries are (19 days) and that
    the fix is entering certificates, not pressing Refresh. "No Date"
    now reads "status unknown until the certificate is entered", with
    43 of them on hire; 25 register assets not in SiteIQ are printed
    as "whereabouts unknown".
