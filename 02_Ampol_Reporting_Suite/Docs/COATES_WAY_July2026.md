# The Coates Way - official wording, branch edition, July 2026

Source: `The_Coates_Way_Branch_Edition_July2026.pdf` beside this file
(Coates presentation "0768_Coates_Preso-BRANCH(TheCoatesWay)_0726_v2",
40 pages, dated 16 Jul 2026). Every line below is lifted from that deck,
page numbers in brackets. This is the wording the suite prints; where a
report says something about the Coates Way, it says it from here.

## Purpose, objective, values (p5)

- **Purpose:** Supporting Australia's growth with leading equipment solutions
- **Objective:** Australia's most trusted equipment partner - delivering Best Service & Value
- **Values:**
  1. **Care Deeply** - Safety & wellbeing first, always
  2. **Customer Focused** - Listen, anticipate, exceed expectations
  3. **Be Our Best** - Accountability, learning, determination
  4. **One Team** - Collaborative, trusted, inclusive
  5. **Competitive Spirit** - Set stretch goals, Be THE best
- **Brand tagline (cover, footers):** Equipped for anything

## What it is (p7-8)

"The Coates Way is our operating model - the framework for how every
person at Coates works, makes decisions and creates value for customers
every single day." One operating system to deliver for customers: People,
Operations, Assets, Financials, connected by the seven traits. "Goodbye
to 'choose your own adventure.' One Coates Way, every branch, every
function, every day."

## The cog - four rings (p9)

1. **Centre - Best Service and Value.** The KPI targets
   (0 · 15 · 20 · 30 · 35 · 60 · 65+) sit here as the non-negotiable
   benchmarks.
2. **Second ring - Performance Pillars (POAF):** People, Operations,
   Assets, Financials.
3. **Third ring - Balanced Scorecard, Execution and Cadence:** how
   performance is measured; reviewed daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly.
4. **Fourth ring - the Seven Traits (teeth of the cog):** the behaviours
   that make the model turn.

## The four pillars, what good looks like in the branch (p11, p35-38)

- **People** - safe, capable teams who know the standard. Safety first,
  every day; clear roles, coaching and follow-through; training plans on
  track; One Team, high standards, no guessing.
- **Operations** - safe, fast, right execution across the hire cycle.
  Run structured daily and weekly meetings; follow up actions and fix
  issues early; deliver on promise; safe, compliant, professional handover.
- **Assets** - fleet ready, available and earning a return. Fleet quality,
  readiness and availability; time utilisation target 65%; Redline
  target <15%; actively manage fleet composition and utilisation.
- **Financials** - disciplined performance that funds the flywheel.
  Revenue and EBIT to budget; ROCE to budget; labour cost to revenue to
  budget; protect value, not just volume.

## Balanced Scorecard and cadence (p12, p33-34)

- RAG on the scorecard: **GREEN - On Track**, **AMBER - At Risk**,
  **RED - Action Needed**.
- "When a metric is red, it requires a documented root cause, corrective
  actions, an owner and a deadline. Persistent red metrics escalate to
  the next level for review and follow-through."
- Cadence: daily checks, weekly PULSE, monthly reviews, quarterly
  strategy reviews. "Build before meetings. Focus on red metrics. Use for
  decisions."
- Avoid: reporting without action, repeated issues, multiple KPI
  versions, moving targets.

## The Seven Traits (p14, p26-32)

1. **Customer Solutions** - understand what the customer is trying to get
   done; the right equipment, service and support to do it safely and
   efficiently. Linked KPIs: NPS 60, win rate 35%.
2. **Targeted Growth** - deliberate focus on the customers, sectors and
   jobs where Coates can win and grow value. ROCE 20%, EBIT 30%.
3. **Performance-led Results** - know the numbers, act early, follow
   through. "Every KPI has a named owner - no orphan numbers. Variance to
   target is explained in plain English. Actions tracked weekly until the
   number is back on." Avoid "red KPIs without an action plan" and "the
   same explanation repeated monthly".
4. **Operational Excellence** - every hire safe, reliable, consistent.
   "Machines pre-checked, clean and ready. On-time deliveries with
   correct kit. Returns processed same day." Linked KPIs: TRIFR 0,
   on-time delivery, Redline <15%, NPS 60. Avoid "unprocessed returns".
5. **Resilient Processes** - follow standard processes; if something
   does not work, fix the process, do not create workarounds. "One
   documented standard process. Workarounds flagged. Fast recovery."
6. **Customer-led Innovation** - frontline feedback, data and tools to
   solve real customer and branch problems.
7. **Ease of Doing Business** - clean handovers, working systems, less
   friction. Linked KPIs: first-call resolution, quote turnaround,
   customer effort score, resolution time <2 hours.

## The Six Operating Disciplines - how we show up (p18)

1. **Safety First** - every job, every load, every person is safe. Stop
   tasks when something isn't right.
2. **Customer Centric** - keep the customer at the centre; own issues
   until resolved; sell value, not price.
3. **Pace** - resolve customer issues within hours; quotes out same day.
4. **Open to Feedback** - ask "what could we do better?"; listen,
   acknowledge and act.
5. **Reduce Friction** - make it easy to do business with Coates and each
   other; flag workarounds and pain points, don't normalise them.
6. **Cadence** - meetings on schedule and structured; actions tracked and
   closed out; regular 1:1 conversations.

## Accountability (p19)

Clear expectations; honest, timely feedback; coach before consequence;
escalate when required.

## Total Value of Hire (p16)

"The customer is not just paying for equipment. They are paying for
availability, reliability, safety, service and certainty." Branch
Manager: "Total value is earned through fleet reliability & availability,
response pace, and consistent execution across every interaction."

## The flywheel (p15)

Accelerates it: fleet reliability & fast response; value-led sales
conversations; strong customer relationships; disciplined reinvestment.
Slows it: equipment downtime; slow quoting & rate-led selling; poor data
quality; inconsistent service.

## What the suite takes from this

- The RAG scale and the red rule (root cause, corrective action, owner,
  deadline) are the suite's RAG band and key strip, word for word.
- Same-day returns (gas monitors) is Trait 4's "returns processed same
  day". The Dräger repair queue as a share of the fleet is the tool
  store's Redline (fleet unavailable) and is shown against the 15% line.
- Every report's closing page names the traits and disciplines it
  serves, in the deck's words. Nothing else from the deck is turned into
  a number: company targets (NPS, ROCE, EBIT, win rate, engagement) are
  not tool-store measures and are not printed as such.
