How we run the count

The wheel beats the blitz
Twenty minutes of bay-walking a day beats a two-day blitz a quarter. Movement resets clocks; the cadence hunts what sits still.
Scan it where it lives
Count an item in its home bay. If it is somewhere else, that is a finding - move it home or fix the register there and then.
The daily three are safety, not stock
A gas monitor missing from the shelf is a bump-test conversation, not a counting error. Same day, every day.
Say what you can't find
An honest MISSING beats a quiet skip. Flag it, log it, and the search starts today instead of at contract end.
