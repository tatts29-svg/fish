  Where these numbers come from
  Two SiteIQ exports, pulled together on 02 Sep 2026, are the only inputs. RENTAL_STOCK is the register - what is on hire, to whom, and what is on the shelf at 18:30. TRANSACTIONS is the movement log - every scan out and scan in since 01 Jan 2026 06:00, one row per monitor per draw with a transaction ID. The store scans twice out and twice in, so a row only exists when a monitor crossed the counter. Nothing is typed in or read from a summary cell; the Excel workbook is not used. A draw is one monitor to one named person once, which is why the yearly count is large - the behaviour measure is the percentage back the same day.
