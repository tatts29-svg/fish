How the rigging fleet is run

Live position, not a snapshot
Status, holder, on-hire date and days held come from the SiteIQ RENTAL_STOCK pull requested 02 Sep 2026 18:30, joined barcode-for-barcode to the register. The workbook decides what is on the register; SiteIQ decides where it is.
Unknown stays unknown
143 register barcodes SiteIQ does not return are printed as not found - whereabouts unknown - never folded into ‘in the store’. Blank test cells print as dashes; nothing is assumed, estimated or copied in.
Longest held, first chased
Customer hire is worked oldest first by company and hirer. Repairs, off-site, out-of-tag and out-of-service custody lines are kept separate, so a quarantined item never reads as a customer’s.
Identity has gaps too
905 rows, 889 distinct barcodes: 1 blank, 14 barcodes repeated on 29 rows, 22 lower-case. The join upper-cases and de-duplicates; the fixes belong in the Master. Rigging gear is Life Saving Rule 5 territory (SEQ-GL-009) - the record has to be real.
