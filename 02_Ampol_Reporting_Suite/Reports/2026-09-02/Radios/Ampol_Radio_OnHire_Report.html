WHAT WE ARE ASKING – PLEASE READ FIRST
  • If a radio or battery is not in use — please return it to the Ampol Tool Store (Coates managed). It is scanned in on the spot and comes straight off this report. Radios returned go back into the available pool for the next shutdown.
  • If it is still in use — bring it past the Ampol Tool Store for a rescan. This is proof of existence: a thirty-second scan verifies the unit is on site, in whose hands, and resets the record. Nothing is taken off you.
  • Site radios are $2,695 each to replace and batteries $246 (new replacement price per Ampol_ToolStore_Pricing.xlsx) — units that can be neither returned nor verified are ultimately chargeable at replacement value under the hire arrangement, applied consistently to all companies. Verification protects everyone from charges for equipment that is actually on site.
  • If anything listed looks incorrect, contact the Ampol Tool Store and we will review and correct the record with you — no charge is finalised without that review.

The moment a unit is scanned, the record updates. Every count on this report is read from the SiteIQ register as at 02 Sep 2026 18:30 - nothing comes from a summary tab.
