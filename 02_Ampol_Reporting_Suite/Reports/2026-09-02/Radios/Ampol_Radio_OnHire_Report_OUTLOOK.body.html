WHAT WE ARE ASKING – RETURN IT OR RESCAN IT
  • Not in use? Return it to the Ampol Tool Store (Coates managed) — it is scanned in on the spot, comes straight off this report, and goes back into the available pool for the next shutdown.
  • Still in use? Bring it past the Ampol Tool Store for a rescan — proof of existence. A thirty-second scan verifies the unit is on site and in whose hands, and resets the record. Nothing is taken off anyone.
  • Site radios are $2,695 each to replace and batteries $246. Units that can be neither returned nor verified are ultimately chargeable at replacement value under the hire arrangement — applied consistently to all companies, and no charge is finalised without review. Verification protects everyone from charges for equipment that is actually on site.
  • Anything look incorrect? Contact the Ampol Tool Store and we will review and correct the record with you.
