 COATES | ON-HIRE REPORT KIT - WHAT'S NEW (makeover, 04 Nov 2026)
 Author: Andrew Fisher | POWERED BY SITEIQ

 Extract this zip over the top of your existing Onhire_Report_Kit
 folder and say Yes to replacing files. Your exports, your LATEST
 workbook, your .xlsm and your Reports folder are not in the zip, so
 nothing of yours is touched. Then press 2_MAKE_THE_REPORT.bat as
 usual.

 THE DATES - the one that matters
 --------------------------------
   Every on-hire item is now dated from the START of its issue
   transaction in SiteIQ, never from the stock record's ON_HIRE_DATE.
   That is what was putting August against gear that went out this
   week. Where the stock record and the transaction agree (most
   items) nothing changes; where they don't, the transaction wins.

   An item with no issue transaction inside the TRANSACTIONS export
   you pulled is shown as UNDATED - in the email, the pack and the
   workbook - and the report says how many and why. Pull TRANSACTIONS
   from the start of the shut and every item dates. The window the
   export covers is printed in the email footer, on the Cover and in
   the pack.

 NEW - THE COMPANY PACK (PDF)
 ----------------------------
   Reports\<today>\<Job> On-Hire Report by Company - <date>.pdf
   A cover with the position and a contents list that links to each
   section, then one section per company: their people A-Z, their
   gear longest-held first, item numbers, the day each item was
   issued, days out, and the consumables they drew this period. Each
   company is a bookmark in Acrobat. Attached to the email next to
   the workbook. One PDF per company in Reports\<today>\By_Company\
   as well, for forwarding a company just their page.

   Built by coates_pdf.py, a small PDF writer that now travels in the
   kit - nothing to install, no browser, no Acrobat needed to make it.

 THE EMAIL - new layout
 ----------------------
   One white sheet on a grey page. Coates masthead, the position in
   words, four flags that say their status in words as well as
   colour, what is attached, then seven slim numbered sections with
   cleaner tables (one bar per table, chips for anything that carries
   a judgement), a lighter company roll, and what I need from you.
   Still tables-and-inline-styles only, no images, so Outlook, Gmail
   and a phone all draw it the same. Still no costs.

 THE WORKBOOK - new look
 -----------------------
   Coates title band (COATES in orange on near-black, orange rule)
   on every tab, charcoal heading rows, plain-English column headings,
   faint zebra, TOTAL rows highlighted on Company Summary, and colour
   that follows the value: ratings and recommendations tinted, On
   Hire tinted, days on hire red past 21 / amber past 13, orange data
   bars on the utilisation columns. Page header and footer on every
   printed tab. Detailed Onhire gains Days on hire, the issue
   transaction behind each date, and the SiteIQ stock date beside it
   for checking. New Cover: facts, headline tiles, what's in each tab,
   what fed it, the ratings legend in colour, what is typed by hand.
   A cost headline that can't be read shows TBC, never $0.

 ONE KIT, EVERY JOB (05 Nov 2026)
 --------------------------------
   The same kit now runs MMG and Weipa. JOB.txt carries the job's
   settings: engagement (fixed rate), shutdown_end (demob countdown
   and the gear-return push from four days out), tidy_names and
   acronyms. COMPANY_ALIASES.txt folds the spellings SiteIQ uses for
   one outfit - only what is written there, never a guess.

   The pack gains THE STORY on page 2: the brief in plain words, the
   team at the counter and their hours to date, how gear is moving
   (same-day returns, kept longer, still out), stocktake discipline
   (items sighted in the last three days, and by whom), customer-owned
   gear logged through the counter, and the standards behind the
   numbers. Every company that touched the counter gets a section,
   holding gear or not - at the end of a shut the activity is the
   story. A fifth dial on the charts page shows stocktake coverage
   when a STOCKTAKE export is in.

   The workbook gains a CUSTOMER GEAR tab - customer- and contractor-
   owned gear logged through the counter (fourteen tabs now). The
   email shows how gear is moving and an Own gear column, and says
   when demob is.

   4_MAKE_OUTLOOK_DRAFT.bat (optional) saves today's email as a NATIVE
   Outlook draft, so the To field has the address book behind it.

   A roster from a previous shutdown is recognised (its last day a
   month or more behind the report) and replaced with the job's seeded
   roster, with a note. MMG's roster and cost breakdown as sent on
   05 Nov 2026 are seeded: fly in Sun 22 Nov, Thomas Maher on days,
   Cody Mitchel on nights, fly out Wed 2 Dec; $90 day / $100 night;
   tooling $1,847.81 and site services $50 a day from 24 Nov to 1 Dec,
   flights $1,500 each way. Labour reads off the roster: $23,040.

 NEW - THE VISUALS
 -----------------
   Page 2 of the company pack is AT A GLANCE: four dials (Coates
   fleet on hire, dated from transactions, consumable lines past 80%,
   items out over 21 days), who is holding gear, tooling and
   consumable lines by rating as donuts, movements by day inside the
   export window, working hardest, and consumables running down
   against the 80% line. Every company section now opens with an
   Items-by-person bar chart. All drawn in the light Coates palette,
   all from the same numbers as the tables.

   The workbook gains a DASHBOARD tab, first after the Cover: six
   tiles, four gauges and four charts drawn by Excel itself from small
   tables at the foot of the tab - live charts you can click, recolour
   or copy into a deck. (The gauges are half-doughnuts; they draw
   properly in Excel, less so in other spreadsheet programs.)

 THE TWO HAND-TYPED TABS - same values, Coates look
 --------------------------------------------------
   Coates Labour and Cost Breakdown are still yours and still carried
   over value for value, formula for formula. They now come through in
   the same look as every other tab: the Coates band, charcoal heading
   rows, light zebra, totals highlighted, dates wide enough to read
   (no more ########), headings frozen, print-ready.

   The roster has been corrected as agreed on 04 Nov 2026: Leon
   Swanepoel on NightShift 18:00-06:00 from Sun 1 Nov to Mon 9 Nov,
   No Shift Tue 10 Nov, Dayshift 06:00-18:00 Wed 11 Nov; Thomas Maher
   on Dayshift 06:00-18:00 throughout. Cost is hours x the rate for the
   shift type - NightShift at the night rate, everything else at the
   day rate - so a changed shift reprices itself. Totals: $23,480
   labour, $40,365.60 all up. The first run replaces the older 'Neon'
   copy of the roster with this one and says so; after that whatever
   you type is what carries over.

   The Cover's labour and cost totals are now worked out from the tab's
   own formulas when Excel has not cached them, so they no longer read
   TBC on a freshly built workbook.
