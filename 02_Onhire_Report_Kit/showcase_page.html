How the store runs

Hire and return checks at the counter

We check SiteIQ after each scan so the hire record shows who took the tool and when it came back.

When we issue gear

  1. Check the gear. We check for damage and missing accessories, make sure the tool is clean and charged where needed, and check that any required tag has more than a week to run.
  2. Scan the hirer. We check the name, status and company on screen. We select Hire only when the details match.
  3. Scan each tool. We scan the tool itself, check that its description matches on screen and watch the count go up by one. We match the final count to the gear on the counter and select Finish before handover.

When gear comes back

  1. Check the open hire. After the first scan, we look at the screen and match the hire record to the gear on the counter.
  2. Inspect the returned gear. We check for damage and missing accessories with the person returning it. We keep damaged gear out of circulation.
  3. Confirm it is off hire. We scan again and look at the screen a second time to confirm the correct item has come off the hirer’s record, including gear held back for damage. If it still shows on hire, we resolve it at the counter.

    Gauge colours

    Orange · Activity
    Gear issued or on hire, store activity and spend to date.
    Mint · Confirmed
    Recorded returns and completed work.
    Amber · Follow-up
    A count, stock level or due item needs checking.
    Coral · Exception
    A confirmed difference or breach of a recorded limit.
    Steel · Reference
    Capacity, an earlier period or a comparison series.
